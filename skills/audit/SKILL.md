# Audit

Take the claim verbatim, review scope, code revision, evidence paths and source identities. Record what author context you saw. Use a fresh reviewer for independent work; an author may deliver only a clearly identified self-check. Use [roles](../../references/roles.md) and the appropriate [discipline lens](../../references/methods.md). Check the original artifact rather than repeating an author's argument. Within approved scope, reconstruct at least the decisive number or logical step by an independent route where feasible.

Check integrity (ground truth, data lineage, shortcuts, fabrication, selection after observing outcomes, frame lock, artifact mismatch), result-to-claim scope, numerical provenance and units, source locators, and the strongest falsifying alternative. For reported numbers give input file/field, formula, tolerances and observed result. For sources independently inspect the passage and its regime. Adjudicate objections and author's response individually. Attach concrete evidence and distinguish executed checks from expert judgment. A script existing on disk proves neither execution nor scientific validity.

Give each applicable review step **PASS**, **WARN** (conclusion needs qualification), **FAIL** (material contradiction or invalid method), **BLOCKED** (missing access/independence), **ERROR** (broken inputs/check), or **NOT_APPLICABLE** with reason. Rank issues by effect on the claim, supply the cheapest discriminating test and state the defensible scope. Carry open steps into the investigation. A reviewer supplies a verdict; the researcher decides the claim.
