# Prepare

Read [study notes](../../references/study-notes.md), [contracts](../../references/teaching-contracts.md) and [specialist assignments](../../references/roles.md). Resolve the existing index and note. A source defines coverage; a learner profile defines what to probe. Read the syllabus, relevant pages and available diagrams. Map each week/objective to a precise section, distinguishing assigned content, extension and missing source. Use the profile helper (`python scripts/profile.py --help` in the plugin root) to check dependencies before adding reusable capabilities. A heading, chat or asset needs no graph node.

Prepare a lesson that a person can read months later: motivate the central question, define notation in context, show a minimal worked case, derive the general result, check assumptions and limits, and connect it to an established concept and a later use. Layer gist and retrieval above full explanations. For a recitation emphasize worked decisions and independent variants; a chapter connects the week-level ideas through one sustained argument. Cite a page, equation, figure or section beside each important scientific statement. Identify your own derivation and verify its intermediate steps. Mark unresolved course coverage explicitly.

Prepare diagnostic items across difficulty levels where useful, a concept-level check, free recall and a genuinely different transfer case. Keep criteria and unrevealed solutions outside live prompts. For visual topics choose a static figure for structure, [ManimGX](../manimgx/SKILL.md) for meaningful motion, or a short verified video as a supplement. Provide a text path through each idea. Inspect renders and file links. Check coverage, math, examples, answer leakage and navigability before marking material ready. If sources or optional media are unavailable, label the affected part and teach the supported portion. Hand off to [learn](../learn/SKILL.md) without changing mastery.
