# Method selection and audit checks

Choose the lens that can change the answer to the question. For empirical results record the observed system, regime, comparator, units, uncertainty and source locator. When transporting a finding to a different system, state the assumption and identify a check that could fail. Method-specific checks below apply both to experiment preparation and to review of actual artifacts.

| Lens | What to check | Evidence to return |
|---|---|---|
| Mathematics | Declare domains and quantifiers, justify each implication and dependency, inspect edge cases, dimensions, limiting cases and possible counterexamples. A numerical example can expose a failure; it cannot prove a universal proposition. | Proof step or counterexample, precise remaining assumptions, computations checked separately |
| Statistics | Define the estimand and independent unit, reconstruct the sample and exclusions, fit partitions, multiplicity and intervals; resample at the correct unit. Account for correlation and zero-event uncertainty. | Raw counts or input lineage, uncertainty method, sensitivity and scope |
| Physics | Match observable, geometry, frame, origin, material, units and regime. Check conservation, limiting behavior and calibration connecting model to measurement. Recompute a pivotal result by another route. | Located constants, applicability, calculations and residual model uncertainty |
| Chemistry | Move explicitly between macroscopic, particulate and symbolic accounts. Check formula identity, elemental and charge balance, mole/mass conversions, phase, temperature, units and standard state. Separate kinetic from thermodynamic claims. | Database entry/version or primary data, numerical check, unresolved chemical interpretation |
| Biology | Identify level and organism, biological versus technical replicates, controls, allocation, batch, sampling, exclusion and multiplicity. Distinguish association, intervention and mechanism. Check assembly/accession version and coordinate conventions for annotations. | Data provenance, scope of system, replicate unit, confounding and limits of transfer |
| Classical ML | Apply the [ML protocol](lenses/ml.md). Inspect related event families across partitions, preprocessing fitted within folds, held-out evaluation, test reuse, baseline/search budgets and uncertainty. | Split IDs and provenance, actual configurations, comparison under comparable resources |
| Quantum ML | Apply the [QML protocol](lenses/qml.md) plus ordinary ML checks. Trace encoding, state preparation, measurements, noise, shot counts, classical processing, resource budget and actual backend. | Input parity, baseline/tuning scope, simulator versus hardware evidence |
| Simulation | Apply the [Geant4 protocol](lenses/geant4.md) where relevant. Establish geometry, range of validity, inputs, scoring and normalization, energy accounting and stochastic streams. | Actual engine, dataset versions, parameters, diagnostic cases, convergence and reproducibility limits |
| Reproducibility | Use the reported revision, environment, data, seed policy and exact command. Reproduce only within approved run scope. Preserve failures and identify missing documentation, data, environment mismatch, nondeterminism or code defect. | Original output and independent output with tolerances and all deviations |

For chemistry or biology teaching, select the domain lens together with the teaching contract. Probe misconceptions with a fresh case. For quantitative findings record what arithmetic or semantics were checked separately from expert judgment. Follow host safety boundaries in all domains.
